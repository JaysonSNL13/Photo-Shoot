Tal — Product Approval

Tal — sample approval. Only products with a sample on hand show here. Approve marks it Ready and adds it to the shoot list, or place an item On Hold with a reason. Held items stay in the To Approve list, marked amber, and return automatically after 7 days or when a new update lands. Items are grouped by the month their photos are needed (need-by = bulk ETA − 14 days), soonest first. When both samples are in hand, pick which to shoot with before approving. Suits show as one row.
